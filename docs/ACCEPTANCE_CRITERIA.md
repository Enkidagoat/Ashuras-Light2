# Acceptance Criteria — Ashura's Light 2

**Status:** Proposed for owner review. Not yet accepted.

## Proposed functional criteria
- AC-01: User can enter site coordinates and date/time and receive Sun and Earth azimuth/elevation.
- AC-02: User can compare a second site or second date.
- AC-03: Every result displays provenance and limitations.
- AC-04: Every result includes the required distinction statements (power, communications, terrain).

## Proposed scientific criteria
- AC-05: Outputs are labelled as centre-direction geometric quantities relative to the local horizon.
- AC-06: No claim equates Sun above horizon with usable power.
- AC-07: No claim equates Earth above horizon with operational communications.
- AC-08: No claim equates geometric visibility with terrain-verified visibility.

## Proposed reproducibility criteria
- AC-09: Recorded inputs and pinned source versions are sufficient to reproduce the numeric result.

## Unresolved
- Final numeric tolerance
- Exact provenance field list
- Hosting and submission requirements

No component will be considered complete without recorded evidence.
